# Mòyuè — The Eternal Descent · Development Report

This report separates what is **implemented**, what was **tested** (and how),
what is **not yet tested**, and what is **blocked** in this development
environment. Nothing is listed as tested unless a test or a recorded check
actually exercised it.

The environment was a Linux container with no GPU, no speakers, no phone and no
Xcode or Android Studio. "Browser" below means headless Chromium through
Playwright 1.56.1, with software WebGL (SwiftShader).

## Project and isolation

* The repository was empty when work began: its first commit is this project.
  No existing files, systems or configuration were touched or removed.
* No game engine is used: no Unity, Unity Editor or Unity MCP, and no Unreal,
  Godot or any other engine. The game is TypeScript (about 15,800 lines in 68
  files). Three.js is used only as a WebGL rendering library. Vite builds it,
  Vitest runs the unit tests and Playwright runs the browser tests.
* Everything carries the project's name. CSS classes start with `moyue-`, the
  storage keys are `moyue_save_v1`, `moyue_save_v1_bak` and `moyue_settings_v1`,
  and the debug handle is `window.__MOYUE__`.
* All content is original and generated by code at runtime: models, textures,
  animation, music, sound, UI, lore and dialogue. Hollow Knight shaped only the
  gameplay philosophy. The four reference images set only the mood, and none of
  them is in the repository.

## IMPLEMENTED

### Movement (src/player, src/physics)
* A 60 Hz fixed simulation step with render interpolation, hit-stop and slow motion.
* Custom kinematic physics: oriented boxes and cylinders in a spatial hash, and a
  capsule character body with snap-to-ground, step-up, slopes, sliding off roofs
  that cannot be walked on, and carrying on moving platforms. The last safe
  ground position is recorded for hazard respawns.
* Run (7.4 m/s), variable-height jump, coyote time (0.10 s), jump buffer
  (0.13 s), fall gravity, apex hang, dash with an air limit and i-frames,
  double jump, wall cling, wall slide and wall jump on carved or root surfaces
  only, and a heal channel.
* A procedural animation rig with squash and stretch, and a verlet scarf.

### Combat (src/combat, src/enemies)
* A three-hit ground chain, air slash, down-slash with pogo, up-slash, soft
  aim, lock-on, a charged spin (Moon-Cleave), a projectile (Lantern Flare) and
  an aerial slam (Bell Strike).
* Hit-stop, camera trauma, recoil, flashes, ink and spark bursts, moonlight
  gain, i-frames after damage, knockback and a damage vignette.
* Creatures: Ink Mite, Lantern Wisp (with an ember spit) and Shieldback (blocks
  from the front). Each attack has a telegraph before it lands.
* The Censer Warden (elite, 2 phases) and the Tolling Abbot (boss, 3 phases).
  Both wait dormant in sealed arenas. Arena waves close barrier gates, and
  altars unseal when an arena is cleared.

### World (src/world, src/art)
* The regions are the Cavern Backdrop, Cicada Threshold, Thousand Lantern
  Terraces, Mistfall Cloister and Crimson Sanctum.
* A procedural architecture kit covers curved hip roofs, pagodas, halls, gates,
  bridges, stairs, lanterns, steles and rock formations. Geometry is batched by
  material and spatial cell, with world-space UVs and baked ambient occlusion.
* The ability gates are listed in `docs/DESIGN.md`. Fall catchers stop the
  player from skipping gates by gliding past them.
* Checkpoints are 4 incense shrines. Resting heals, saves, sets the respawn
  point and resets ordinary enemies. Dying returns the player to the last
  shrine with nothing lost.
* NPCs are Keeper Weng (dialogue, shop, Lantern Flare) and the pilgrim Xun
  (3 appearances). The game has an opening cutscene, an ending, and region
  title cards.

### Rendering (src/fx)
* A post chain of bright pass, blur pyramid, ACES tone mapping, grading,
  vignette and grain. Height fog is patched into every material, including
  custom shaders.
* Rim-lit character materials, ink outlines, instanced glows, a fixed-size
  light pool and GPU ambient particles.
* Quality presets are low, medium, high and auto. Auto lowers the resolution
  when frames are slow. Presets change the pixel ratio, bloom, shadows, light
  count, particles and draw distance.
* Draw-call controls: regions outside a view distance are hidden (a view volume
  shows the Sanctum from the Descent Well), entities beyond 72 m are hidden, the
  cavern shell uses coarse batches, and character and prop parts are merged.

### Mobile and input (src/input, src/ui)
* A floating left stick with dead zone and sensitivity settings, and a fixed
  mode. Dragging on the right side turns the camera. Buttons sit in a thumb arc
  (Jump, Strike, Dash, Moon, Focus, plus a context button for talk, rest, read
  and take), with pause and map at the top right. Sliding a thumb between
  buttons presses the new one, and several touches work at once.
* Safe areas are read from `env(safe-area-inset-*)` and cover notches, the
  Dynamic Island, the home indicator and Android navigation bars.
* A layout editor lets players drag each button and set its size, plus global
  size and opacity.
* Keyboard and mouse (with pointer lock) and gamepad (Gamepad API) are also
  supported. Hints use the wording of the input in use, and touch devices start
  with touch wording.
* Menus: title (Continue or New Descent), pause, settings (Controls, Camera,
  Audio and Graphics tabs, including the touch layout editor and erasing the
  save), a journal (lanterns, fragments, jade, vessels, play time, techniques
  and steles read) and the map.

### Audio (src/audio)
* WebAudio synthesis only, with no sample files.
* Music is generative, with a track for each region plus battle, boss,
  opening and ending. It uses Chinese pentatonic modes (gong, shang, jue, zhi,
  yu) played by zither, flute, bowed-string, drone, bell and drum voices.
* Ambience beds (cave, terraces, water, sanctum) carry randomised detail
  sounds.
* 70 synthesised sound effects are positioned in 3D. The engine limits how
  often each effect can repeat and caps the number of voices.
* Buses for master, music, effects and ambience feed a reverb built from a
  generated cave impulse response.

### Save system (src/save)
* The save carries an FNV checksum and a backup slot, and falls back to the
  backup when the primary copy is corrupted.
* Every field is sanitised on load. Settings are stored separately.
* Storage access is wrapped, so private-browsing failures do not crash the game.

### Distribution
* `npm run build` produces a static web build with a PWA manifest and icons.
* `scripts/build-single.mjs` inlines the build into one HTML file of about
  924 KB, or 255 KB gzipped. `--fragment` strips the document skeleton for
  hosts that wrap the page themselves.
* The README describes how to wrap the build with Capacitor for the App Store
  and Google Play.

## TESTED

### Automated: unit and headless simulation (`npm test`, 59 tests, all passing)
* **Controller (18 tests).** Idle without drift, acceleration and stopping,
  25° ramps, step-up, walls, jump height, variable jump, coyote time, jump
  buffer, running jump distance, dash distance and air-dash limit, double jump,
  cling and wall jump on climbable walls only, platform carry, ramp crests,
  roof slide-off, and chimney climbing.
* **Traversal (11 tests).** Each region's real collision geometry is built in
  Node, and an autopilot drives the real controller. The tests prove the
  critical path from tomb to ending, and prove that each gate is impassable
  without its ability. They also cover the hidden-ledge fragment, the pagoda
  shortcut (no softlock), the lift, the thorn-pool pogo and the seal break.
* **Combat (4 tests).** Hit volumes for the forward slash, the turned swing,
  the down slash and the spin.
* **Save (7 tests).** Round-trip, no save, fallback to the backup, both copies
  corrupt, hostile fields, clamping, and a settings round-trip.
* **Spear, IK and hair (14 tests).** See the protagonist section below.
* **Effects and camera (5 tests).** The light flash borrows a light slot,
  fades and hands it back, and slows with slow motion. The enemy-defeat
  effect scales with enemy size and is gone within about a second. The combat
  camera framing clears the protagonist's mask for an enemy 1.5 m ahead.

### Automated: end-to-end in the browser (`npm run e2e`, 15 tests, all passing)
* The title screen boots with no console errors.
* A new game plays the opening, and the keyboard moves the player.
* After the full opening with no input, the scripted camera hands control back
  with the player in view. This test was added for a bug found in this session.
* On-screen controls stay inside the safe area on 6 emulated devices: iPhone 15
  and iPhone SE in landscape, an Android phone, an iPad, an Android tablet, and
  a phone in portrait. The safe-area insets are injected.
* Touch: the stick moves the player while jump and strike are pressed with
  other touches, and a camera drag turns the view.
* On a touch device, the first hints use touch wording. This test was also
  added for a bug found in this session.
* The touch pause button pauses, and resume returns to play.
* Settings survive a reload. Resting at a shrine saves, and Continue restores
  the shrine, abilities and pickups. A corrupted save falls back to the backup.

### Recorded checks in the browser (scripts in `scripts/`)
* **Screenshots.** Title, opening, tomb, overlook, Terraces, Mistfall and
  Sanctum were captured and reviewed after each visual change. Issues found
  this way and fixed: an oversaturated tomb light, a lime-coloured dais, and a
  blank screen after the opening.
* **Autopilot fights (`combat-check.mjs`).** Mites were killed, the Cloud Step
  arena was cleared and its altar unsealed, and the Censer Warden and Tolling
  Abbot were defeated with their flags set and altars unsealed. There were no
  page errors. The last run was after the final changes.
* **Ending.** Falling through the broken seal plays the ending text and sets
  `ending_seen`, with no errors.
* **Audio (`audio-check.mjs`).** The AudioContext runs at 44.1 kHz, all 71
  effects play through the graph, and there are no errors. This check can
  hear nothing. A level measurement on the effects bus showed the new defeat
  chime peaking at 0.30 against 0.82 for the kill impact it sits on.
* **Single-file build.** It boots to the title and into play on a desktop
  viewport and on an emulated iPhone 13 in landscape, with no errors. The
  camera sees the player after the opening.
* **Draw calls (`drawcalls.mjs`, medium quality).** About 150 calls at the
  Terraces, Mistfall and Sanctum points measured. About 360 at the Threshold
  overlook, the widest vista, which drops to about 215 on low quality. Before
  the optimisations, Mistfall used about 268.

## Protagonist integration pass

The user supplied a new protagonist: a front three-quarter render and a
sheet of eight poses. No 3D file was supplied. The character was rebuilt in
code to match the images; details are in [`PROTAGONIST.md`](PROTAGONIST.md).
The first attempt did not follow the reference closely. The user stopped it,
and it was corrected by comparing renders side by side with the reference.

**IMPLEMENTED**
- **Model.** Mask-head, hair, buns and pins, cloak, chest plate, sash,
  two-tier skirt, pauldrons, arms, legs, and a spear with a phoenix pommel,
  ribbons and a flame head. All are built to the measured proportions.
- **Integration with the existing player system.** Rig joint names, the
  controller, the collision capsule, the camera, abilities and saves are
  unchanged.
- **Animation.**
  - Poses for idle, walk and run, take-off, rise, fall, landing, dash, wall
    cling, hurt, death, heal, slam and charge.
  - Gestures for Lantern Flare (a palm strike), interaction (a bow of the
    head) and learning a technique (the spear raised).
  - Attacks: two spear sweeps, a lunging thrust (reference pose 6), an
    overhead cut (reference pose 7), down and up thrusts, and the charged
    spin.
  - The spear is carried on the back and drawn into the hand for combat,
    with two-bone IK for the hands.
  - Secondary motion: simulated hair, spring-driven cloak and ribbons.
- **Hitbox sync.** The spear's reach now sets the melee ranges (slash 1.75,
  air 1.7, finisher 1.85 m, previously 2.05, 2.05 and 2.35), and the slash
  arcs are gold to match the spearhead.
- **Fixes.**
  - The rig placed the hips at ground level, burying the old character up
    to its robe and the new one by 0.13 m. Fixed.
  - Shaders now compile during the loading screen instead of on first sight.
    The new character's materials had added about 0.6 s to the freeze on the
    first frame of play.

**TESTED**
- **Unit tests (54, all passing).** They include:
  - spear-tip-versus-hit-volume sync for every attack;
  - two-bone IK accuracy;
  - hair-chain stability, ground contact, and streaming when running.

  The controller and traversal tests are unchanged and pass.
- **Browser comparison.** Side-by-side renders against the reference and
  the pose sheet: three-quarter, front, four orthographic views, and the
  thrust and overhead poses.
- **In the game.** Idle from four angles, slash, jump, dash and run
  captured in slow motion.
- **End-to-end.** The movement test in `boot.spec.ts` now measures one
  second of game time instead of 2.5 s of wall-clock time. In this container
  the first frame of play can take about 2.7 s under software rendering. That
  delay predates this pass, and it made the test flaky.

**NOT YET TESTED**
- The character on a real phone: readability, frame rate, and the cost of
  simulating the hair and cloak on mobile CPUs.
- Whether the model is close enough to the reference in the user's eyes.
  This needs the user's confirmation.

### Second part: enemy defeat and camera

After the corrected model was shown, the user said to continue. This part
touches only enemies, effects and the camera. The protagonist model is
unchanged.

**IMPLEMENTED**
- **Colourful enemy defeat** (`Effects.defeat`, `Enemy.die` and
  `Enemy.render`). It lasts about half a second and follows the pattern
  "dark world → a colourful moment → back to darkness":
  - a white core flash and a violet halo;
  - two fine rings (turquoise and violet);
  - streaking sparks in cyan, blue, violet, magenta, soft gold and
    turquoise, drawn as one instanced mesh;
  - a few motes that rise and fade;
  - a real light that briefly borrows a light-pool slot;
  - the creature pops and then stretches upward and dissolves into violet
    light;
  - a short crystalline chime.

  The effect scales with enemy height, so bosses get a larger one. The old
  dark ink burst is kept, smaller. The light and particles slow down with
  slow motion.
- **Camera.**
  - The base distance is 6.0 m (previously 6.6) so the character reads
    larger on a phone.
  - **Combat framing.** When a living enemy is within 4 m the camera tilts
    to 0.46 rad (normally 0.30). Lock-on uses the same pitch (previously
    0.34). Otherwise a small enemy at spear range sat behind the large mask
    and mane: the line of sight crossed the character at 0.83 m, just below
    the mask top at 1.0 m. It now crosses at about 1.05 m.

**TESTED**
- 5 new unit tests (see above).
- **Browser, in slow motion (4 % speed), a real kill.** Frames were captured
  both close up and from the gameplay camera: flash, sparks, rings, dissolve,
  and back to dark.
- **Autopilot fight.** 2 kills, no errors.
- **Full suites.** 59 unit tests and 15 E2E tests pass, and the build
  succeeds.

**NOT YET TESTED**
- The chime heard by a person. Only its level was measured.
- The effect's cost on a real phone. It adds up to 96 instanced streaks in
  one draw call and one borrowed light.
- Whether the combat pitch feels comfortable to a player.

## NOT YET TESTED

* **Real phones and tablets.** Nothing has run on a physical iPhone, iPad or
  Android device. That includes touch feel and latency, real multi-touch,
  haptics, real notch and navigation-bar insets, rotation, backgrounding and
  resuming, thermal throttling and battery use.
* **Real GPU performance.** Software WebGL runs at 10–14 fps here, which says
  nothing about frame rate on a device. The auto-quality scaler has only been
  exercised on this slow renderer, where it drops to its lowest scale.
* **Listening.** No sound, music or mix has been heard by anyone. Loudness
  balance, whether the music is pleasant, and clicks or pops are all unknown.
* **Gamepad.** The Gamepad API mapping has not been used with a real controller.
* **A full human playthrough.** Simulations and autopilots cover each section,
  but nobody has played from start to end. Difficulty, pacing, camera comfort,
  readability of the telegraphs and total play time are unknown.
* **Safari and Firefox.** Only Chromium has been run.
* **The published page.** It was built and published, but whether it loads
  inside the claude.ai viewer (WebGL, audio unlock, pointer lock) has not been
  checked.
* **The PWA.** Installing it to the home screen has not been tried.

## BLOCKED (cannot be done in this environment)

* **Native iOS and Android apps.** These need Xcode, Android Studio, signing
  certificates and store accounts. The Capacitor steps are in the README.
* **Device and store testing** (TestFlight, Play Console). This needs hardware
  and accounts.
* **Audio evaluation.** This needs someone to listen.

## Known limitations

* The game is a vertical slice: 4 regions, 3 creature types, 1 elite and
  1 boss, and 6 techniques. The world is designed to be extended; region
  definitions live in `src/world/regions`.
* Shadows are only on for high quality. Low and medium use baked ambient
  occlusion and the light pool.
* The widest vista, the Threshold overlook, is the heaviest view at about
  360 draw calls on medium. If older Android phones struggle there, auto or low
  quality hides the distant Mistfall.
* Hints and lore are in English only.

## Suggested first device checks

1. Run `npm run dev` and open the LAN address on a phone in
   landscape. Start with Settings → Graphics → Auto.
2. Play the Threshold. Check the stick dead zone, jump feel, and holding the
   stick while tapping JUMP then STRIKE. Check the camera drag.
3. Check the safe areas on a notched iPhone and on an Android phone with
   on-screen navigation.
4. Rest at the first shrine, close the tab, reopen, and choose Continue.
5. Listen with headphones in each region and in the Cloud Step arena.
